# Assessment Session 1: Upper Body Cable Discovery

**Date:** 2026-03-14 (Saturday)
**Athlete:** Harrison, 7'0", 330 lbs, 15 months detrained
**Goal:** Build load-velocity profiles on 3-4 key cable exercises
**Session type:** Discovery / Assessment
**Time limit:** 40-50 minutes
**Hard rules:** No set above RPE 7. No set below 0.40 m/s mean velocity. Stop discovery if velocity categorizes as "grinding."

## Pre-Session

### Voltra Setup
- Both Voltras connected, Weight Training mode
- Map cable height positions for your rack (note which notch = sternum, which = navel, which = overhead)
- Have D-handles, V-handle, straight bar, and rope attachments ready

### Warmup (5-7 min)
- 2 min general movement (arm circles, band pull-aparts if available, bodyweight squats)
- Light cable work: each Voltra at 20 lbs, 10 reps each of: chest press, row, curl, pushdown
- Purpose: get blood flowing, check cable positions, practice the movements

## Exercise 1: Cable Chest Press (Bilateral, Standing)

### Setup
- Cable height: sternum level (for 7'0" this is likely 4-6 notches above the rack's default "mid")
- Attachment: D-handles
- Position: stand 2-3 feet forward of the cable posts
- Feet: staggered stance for stability

### Discovery Protocol
- **Estimated 1RM input: 95 lbs per side**
- Discovery ramp: 30 (29), 50 (48), 65 (62), 75 (71), 85 (81) lbs
- 3 reps per load, 2-3 min rest between
- Record: mean velocity, peak velocity, RPE

### Decision Points
- If velocity at 81 lbs > 0.55 m/s: bump estimate to 120 and test 85% (102 lbs)
- If velocity at 81 lbs < 0.40 m/s: estimate was too high, profile is complete
- Target: identify the load where velocity = 0.40-0.55 m/s (hypertrophy working zone)

### Working Sets (post-discovery)
- 2 sets × 8 reps at the discovered hypertrophy load
- RPE target: 5-6 (leave 4-5 reps in reserve)
- Tempo: 1s concentric, 3s eccentric, 0s pause top, 1s pause bottom
- Focus: controlled eccentric, feel the stretch at full extension

## Exercise 2: Cable Row (Bilateral, Seated or Standing)

### Setup
- Cable height: low-mid (navel level when standing, or use low position with Pegasus seat)
- Attachment: V-handle or straight bar
- If seated: sit on Pegasus or floor with legs extended

### Discovery Protocol
- **Estimated 1RM input: 95 lbs per side**
- Same 5-load ramp
- 3 reps per load

### Working Sets
- 2 sets × 8 reps at hypertrophy load
- Tempo: 1s concentric, 2s eccentric, 1s squeeze at peak contraction
- Focus: full scapular retraction, controlled release to stretch

## Exercise 3: Cable Lat Pulldown (Bilateral)

### Setup
- Cable height: highest position on both posts
- Attachment: wide bar or D-handles
- Seat: Pegasus with knee pad engaged
- For 7'0": verify full arm extension at top doesn't hit cable limit

### Discovery Protocol
- **Estimated 1RM input: 75 lbs per side**
- Same 5-load ramp
- 3 reps per load

### Working Sets
- 2 sets × 10 reps at hypertrophy load
- Tempo: 1s concentric, 3s eccentric (slow negative is key for lat growth)
- Focus: initiate with lats not biceps, full stretch at top

## Exercise 4 (Optional): Cable Curl (Bilateral)

### Setup
- Cable height: lowest position
- Attachment: straight bar or EZ curl attachment
- Stand upright, elbows at sides

### Protocol (simplified — not full discovery)
- Ramp: 30 lbs × 8, 40 lbs × 8, 50 lbs × 6
- Find a moderate working weight
- 2 sets × 12 reps at RPE 5-6
- Tempo: 1s concentric, 2s eccentric, 1s squeeze at top

## Coach Notes

### What I'm Looking For
1. **Load-velocity profiles** for chest press, row, pulldown — this tells me your current strength curve per exercise
2. **Velocity consistency** — are rep-to-rep velocities stable or erratic? Erratic = neuromuscular re-learning needed
3. **ROM patterns** — does position data show consistent range or does it shorten under load?
4. **RPE calibration** — how does your subjective RPE compare to the velocity-derived RPE? This tells me how well you can self-assess right now

### What I'm NOT Looking For
- Max strength numbers (we'll get those in 3-4 weeks)
- Perfect form (movement re-patterning takes sessions, not minutes)
- Muscle fatigue / pump (too early for that stimulus)

### Red Flags (stop the session)
- Sharp pain (vs muscle burn — burn is fine)
- Dizziness or lightheadedness
- Any set where velocity drops more than 30% within the set
- Cumulative fatigue: if discovery set 4+ velocity at a given load is >15% slower than earlier at same load
