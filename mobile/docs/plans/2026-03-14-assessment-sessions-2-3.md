# Assessment Sessions 2 & 3: Lower Body + Overhead/Accessories

## Session 2: Lower Body + Posterior Chain (Monday or Tuesday)

**Prerequisites:** Session 1 DOMS should be manageable (may still be present — that's OK)
**Time:** ~50 minutes
**Equipment:** SSB bar, Voltras (low cable), deadlift platform, bench

### Warmup (7-8 min)
- 3 min walking or light cycling
- Bodyweight squats × 10, hip circles × 10 each direction
- SSB bar only × 8 (groove the squat pattern)
- Light cable pull-throughs × 10 (hip hinge warmup)

### Exercise 1: SSB Squat (Barbell — Manual Log)

Estimated current 1RM: ~200 lbs (down from 300×3, e1RM ~330, assuming 35-40% loss)

| Set | Load | Reps | Purpose |
|-----|------|------|---------|
| 1 | Bar (65 lbs) | 8 | Pattern check — knees tracking, depth comfort |
| 2 | 95 lbs | 6 | Feel for load |
| 3 | 135 lbs | 5 | Moderate territory |
| 4 | 165 lbs | 3 | Only if set 3 was RPE < 6 |
| 5 | 185 lbs | 2 | Only if set 4 was RPE < 7 |

**7'0" notes:**
- Wider stance (just outside shoulder width), toes out 30-45°
- Heel elevation (squat shoes or 1-1.5" wedge)
- SSB forward load shift compensates for long femurs
- Depth target: hip crease at knee level, not full ATG
- **Stop at RPE 7. Period.**

### Exercise 2: Cable RDL (Voltra, Low Cable, Bilateral)

Estimated cable 1RM: ~80 lbs per side (extrapolated from 495 conventional → ~330 current → ~80 cable per side)

- Discovery mode: estimated 1RM **80 lbs per side**
- Ramp: 24, 40, 52, 60, 68 lbs
- 3 reps per load, focus on hip hinge and hamstring stretch
- 2 working sets × 8 at hypertrophy load

**Setup:** Low cables, straight bar or rope between legs, stand facing away from machine

### Exercise 3: Cable Split Squat (Voltra, Low Cable, Unilateral)

Estimated: ~40-50 lbs per side

- Discovery mode: estimated 1RM **45 lbs per side**
- Ramp: 14, 23, 29, 34, 38 lbs
- 3 reps per load per leg
- 1 working set × 10 per leg at hypertrophy load

**7'0" notes:** Extra long stride. Front shin perpendicular to floor. Cable low, D-handles at sides.

### Exercise 4: Cable Leg Curl (Voltra, Low Cable)

- No discovery needed — use 20-30 lbs
- 2 sets × 12-15 reps
- Ankle strap, standing or prone on bench
- Focus: hamstring curl through full ROM
- This substitutes for the missing leg curl machine

| Exercise | Equipment | Discovery | Working Sets | Total Sets |

| Cable RDL | Voltra | Yes | 2 | 7 |
| Cable Split Squat | Voltra | Yes | 2 (per leg) | 8 |

## Session 3: Overhead + Accessories (Wednesday or Thursday)

**Time:** ~45 minutes

### Warmup (5 min)
- Shoulder dislocations with band/broomstick × 10
- Light cable lateral raises × 10 each arm
- Light OHP: bar × 10

### Exercise 1: Barbell OHP (or Cable OHP)

Estimated current 1RM: ~130 lbs (down from 195, assuming 35% loss)

**Option A: Barbell OHP (Manual Log)**
| Set | Load | Reps | Purpose |
|-----|------|------|---------|
| 1 | Bar (45) | 8 | Groove the press |
| 2 | 65 lbs | 6 | Light working |
| 3 | 85 lbs | 5 | Moderate |
| 4 | 105 lbs | 3 | Only if RPE < 7 |
| 5 | 115 lbs | 2 | Only if RPE < 7 |

**Option B: Cable OHP (Voltra, Low Cable)**
- Discovery mode: estimated 1RM **65 lbs per side**
- Gets us a cable overhead press LV profile
- D-handles from low cables, press overhead

**7'0" note:** Standing OHP is fine but the press distance is very long. Seated removes stability demand. Cable OHP from low cables lets us adjust start position.

### Exercise 2: Cable Fly (Voltra, Low-to-Mid, Stretch Emphasis)

- Discovery mode: estimated 1RM **45 lbs per side**
- Ramp: 14, 23, 29, 34, 38 lbs
- **ROM limit: 80% of full stretch in week 1** (enormous wingspan at 7'0" means extreme pec stretch — protect the shoulder insertion)
- 2 working sets × 12 at light-moderate load
- Tempo: 1s concentric, 3s eccentric, 1s stretch pause

### Exercise 3: Cable Lateral Raise (Voltra, Low Cable, Unilateral)

- Discovery mode: estimated 1RM **25 lbs per side** (lateral raises are humbling)
- Cross-body setup: stand sideways to one Voltra, D-handle in far hand
- 2 working sets × 12-15 per arm

### Exercise 4: Cable Overhead Tricep Extension (Voltra, Low Cable)

- No full discovery — ramp 20, 30, 40 lbs
- 2 sets × 12 at moderate
- Face away from machine, extend overhead
- Stretch emphasis on the long head

### Exercise 5: Cable Pullover (Voltra, High Cable)

- Discovery mode: estimated 1RM **60 lbs per side**
- High cable, straight bar or rope
- 2 working sets × 10
- Focus: lat stretch at full extension

### Volume Summary

| Exercise | Equipment | Discovery | Working Sets | Total Sets |
|----------|-----------|-----------|-------------|-----------|
| OHP | Barbell or Voltra | Manual/Discovery | 2-3 | 5 |
| Cable Fly | Voltra | Yes | 2 | 7 |
| Cable Lateral Raise | Voltra | Yes | 2 per arm | 7 |
| Cable OH Tri Extension | Voltra | No | 2 | 4 |
| Cable Pullover | Voltra | Yes | 2 | 7 |
| **Total** | | | **10-11** | **30** |
